# MATH 346 Working Context

Before changing course content or assessment design:

1. Read `PROJECT-STATUS.md`.
2. Read `instructor-preview.qmd` and `instructor-progress.qmd`.
3. Treat items under **Open Decisions** as proposals, not approved policy.
4. Update the status record and the public progress page after a substantive
   decision or milestone.
